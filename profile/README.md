## Hi there 👋

We are [Kern AI](https://www.kern.ai/company/about) - part of the Accompio group and a team of ambitious data engineers focusing on how modern artificial intelligence can change our daily work.

Our open-source product is [refinery](https://github.com/code-kern-ai/refinery), the data scientist's open-source choice to scale, assess and maintain natural language data.
